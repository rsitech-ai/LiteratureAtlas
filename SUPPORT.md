# Support

Public issues may be used for reproducible, non-sensitive bugs and feature
requests. Support is best-effort with no response-time or compatibility
guarantee.

Include the commit or version, macOS version, architecture, reproduction steps,
and sanitized logs. Never attach research documents, generated `Output/`, full
document paths, credentials, signing/notarization material, crash logs containing
private content, security findings, or conduct reports.

Modified/community distributions, Apple signing, and notarization are the
distributor's responsibility. Send security or conduct reports privately to
`info@rsitech.ai`.
