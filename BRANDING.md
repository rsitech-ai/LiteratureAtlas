# Branding and community builds

Community distributions must use a distinct product name and a bundle identifier
controlled by their distributor. They must replace any future official icon,
support/privacy links, update configuration, signing metadata, and release
metadata that could imply an official build.

Community builds must not reuse official checksums or notarization evidence, or
claim endorsement by the project or Apple. The current repository has no
approved production icon artwork.

This is a technical redistribution policy, not a claim of registered trademark
rights. See [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md).
