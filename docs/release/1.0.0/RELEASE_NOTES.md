# LiteratureAtlas 1.0.0 Release Notes — Draft

Meet LiteratureAtlas, a private research workspace that turns your PDF and Markdown library into an explorable knowledge universe.

## Highlights

- Import research documents from a folder you choose.
- Explore papers, topics, relationships, and open questions in the Knowledge Universe.
- Ask questions across your corpus and inspect cited local evidence.
- Turn reading into insights and structured research projects.
- Review local analytics and export durable research artifacts.
- Keep source-folder access read-only on macOS and process supported model tasks on device.

## Platform availability

- macOS 26.0 or later.
- iPadOS 26.0 or later on iPad.
- Requires a supported device with Apple Intelligence enabled and the on-device model available.

Final wording, localization, support links, and claims require product-owner approval before submission.
